Company ranks

Know who pays before you apply

Ranks are set by the total amount a company has paid to testers, together with how fast it reviews and pays. A higher rank means a longer track record.

Quick answer

TGR TestWorks ranks companies in ten tiers: Diamond Elite, Platinum Prime, Gold Premier, Emerald Trusted, Ruby Pro, Sapphire Plus, Amethyst, Bronze, Steel and Starter. The rank depends on the total a company has paid testers and how fast it reviews and pays.

Diamond EliteHighest payout · Fastest pay

Top of the table. This company has paid testers more than almost anyone on TGR TestWorks and reviews and pays among the fastest.

Platinum PrimeTop payout partner

A leading payer. Large total payouts to testers and a very quick review and payment time.

Gold PremierPremium payer

A premium payer. Strong payouts to testers and reliable turnaround on every work.

Emerald TrustedTrusted payer

A trusted payer. Testers are paid steadily and on time for approved work.

Ruby ProConsistent payer

A consistent payer with a healthy record of payouts to testers.

Sapphire PlusReliable payer

A reliable payer. Approved work is paid and the review time is fair.

AmethystRising payer

A rising payer. Payouts are growing and more testers are being paid every month.

BronzeVerified payer

A verified payer. The company has paid testers and passed TGR checks.

SteelActive payer

An active payer. The company is running works and paying testers.

StarterNew payer

A new payer. The company has just started on TGR TestWorks and its record is still building.

What you see on a company

Paid out

The total amount the company has paid to testers.

Testers

How many testers have worked with the company.

Pays in

The usual review and payout time, such as 10 days.
