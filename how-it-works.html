How TGR TestWorks works

A simple path from creating your account to money in your UPI app. Here is every step.

Quick answer

To earn on TGR TestWorks: 1) create a free account, 2) find a test in Explore and apply, 3) check your status in My Tests, 4) do the work and send honest bug reports, 5) withdraw to UPI once your balance reaches ₹100. You must be 18+ and use Android 5.0 or newer.

Before you start

Android 5.0 or newer

The app runs on any Android phone with Android 5.0 and above.

18 years or older

You enter your true date of birth. People under 18 cannot apply.

One device, one account

Only one account can be created on a single device.

1. Create your account

Sign-up takes five short steps. Use your real name and keep your password private.

1

Personal details

Full name, username and date of birth.

2

Contact

Your email address and phone number.

3

Location

Your country and city.

4

Testing profile

Your experience level and what you like to test.

5

Security

Set a password and accept the terms. You get a Reference ID once you submit.

2. Find and apply for a test

  • Open the Explore tab and search by test or company name.
  • Filter by App, Web, Game or Software.
  • Open a job to see the company, the type of work and the reward.
  • Read the terms of that work to the end, tick the box and apply.
  • Each tester can apply for a work only once.

3. Follow your status

Every application shows its status in the My Tests tab.

Pending

Your application is waiting for the company to review it.

Approved

You are selected. Complete the work and the reward is added after it is checked.

Not selected

Places are limited. You can apply for other jobs right away.

4. Test and report bugs

  • Do the work exactly as described on the work page.
  • Report only what you really found. Fake or repeated reports are not accepted.
  • Keep the company's product private. Do not share or post it.
  • The reward is added to your earnings after your work is checked and approved.

5. Get paid

Once your balance reaches ₹100 you can withdraw to your UPI app. See how payouts work

Ready to start?

Download the app and create your account in a few minutes.
