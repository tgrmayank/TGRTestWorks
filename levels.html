Levels and badges

Complete works, level up

Every approved work counts toward your next badge. Badges you have not unlocked yet show how to earn them.

Quick answer

TGR TestWorks has seven levels: Newcomer (on sign-up), Explorer (5 works), Bug Hunter (15), Pro Tester (40), Expert (100), Master (250) and Legend (500). Two special badges, Partner and Officer, cannot be earned by counting works.

Special badges

These two cannot be earned by counting works.

PartnerSpecial badge

Awarded to people who work with a TGR TestWorks partner company.

OfficerSpecial badge

Awarded to TGR TestWorks officers and team members who run the app.

Where you see your badge

  • Under your name on the Profile tab (the Partner badge is shown there in the screenshots).
  • On the Badges page in the app, with locked and unlocked badges.
  • On the Leaderboard, next to your rank.
