Features

Everything the app does

Built for testers in India. Clear status for every test and every rupee.

Quick answer

TGR TestWorks has five tabs: Home, Explore, My Tests, Earnings and Profile. It includes job search with App, Web, Game and Software filters, bug reports, a wallet with transaction history, UPI withdrawals, refer and earn, a leaderboard, levels and badges, notifications and in-app support.

Explore jobs

Search by test or company name and filter by App, Web, Game or Software.

My Tests

See Pending, Approved and Not selected counts and the reward from approved tests.

Clear work terms

Every work has its own terms. Read to the end and tick to apply.

Bug reports

Send reports from inside the app and earn a reward when they are accepted.

Wallet and earnings

Total earned, withdrawn and recent activity, with a hide-balance option.

UPI withdrawals

Withdraw from ₹100 to the UPI app you already use.

Transaction history

Every credit and withdrawal with date, time and status.

Refer and earn

Share your code and earn when friends complete their tasks.

Leaderboard

See how you rank among other testers.

Levels and badges

Seven levels from Newcomer to Legend, plus Partner and Officer badges.

Companies and ranks

Top companies show paid out, testers and payout time, with a rank badge.

Notifications

Get notified when a test is approved or money is credited.

Help and support

Raise tickets in the app or reach us on WhatsApp and email.

Profile and security

Edit your profile, add a photo and change your password.

A closer look

Home

Your wallet balance and notifications sit at the top. Below are banners and four quick actions: Withdraw, History, Available tests and Companies. Top companies show how much they have paid, how many testers they have and how fast they pay.

Earnings

See total earned, amount withdrawn and your recent activity, with a date and status on every line. Hide your balance with the eye icon when you are in public. The Refer and Earn banner is right below.

Profile

Edit your details, add a photo, change your password, manage notifications and open Help and Support. Your badge sits right under your name.

See it on your phone

Free to download. Works on Android 5.0 and above.
